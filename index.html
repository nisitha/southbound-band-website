Our Story

The Sound & The Spirit

Southbound is Northern California's premier 6-piece Southern Rock revue — a live jukebox of the greatest songs ever recorded, delivered with true arena-rock energy.

Formed in 2018 in the Greater Sacramento area, Southbound has spent years honing the twin-lead guitar harmonies, the organ-driven Hammond grooves, and the powerful three-part vocal blend that defined the classic Southern Rock era. Whether it's the Ryman-worthy emotion of an Allman Brothers ballad or the full-tilt boogie of a Lynyrd Skynyrd anthem, this band plays every note like it matters.

The band brings 6 seasoned musicians to every stage — from intimate club rooms and outdoor summer festivals to full-scale theater productions. Southbound can perform up to 4 complete sets without repeating a song, covering the essential canon of Southern Rock from the 1970s through today.

The Lineup

Meet the Band

Six seasoned musicians. One unstoppable sound.

Lead Vocals

Scott Baxter

Scott Baxter is a multi-instrumentalist and veteran of hundreds of shows along the Texas Gulf coast, including gigs with rock and roll pioneer Bo Diddley and South Texas blues legend Rocky Benton. Scott has been the face of Southbound since 2023.

Guitars & Vocals

Dean Fisher

It was the release of Freebird nearly 50 years ago that triggered Dean's desire to pick up the guitar. That musical journey has allowed Dean to play with amazing musicians and form a brotherhood much like an MC! Living the dream with Southbound.

Bass Guitar & Vocals

Mike Cochran

Mike decided at age 15 he was going to play bass guitar. Ten years later, he and Dean Fisher formed Savannah Blue. 28 years later, Mike and Dean have morphed those Southern Rock influences into Southbound: "I've learned so much from every musician."

Guitar & Vocals

Rusty Chain

Growing up outside Washington DC, Rusty steeped in blues-rock with Danny Gatton and Jimmy Thackery. Later in Sacramento he formed Chain-Lightning and the Bad Catz. Today, Rusty's gritty blues-soaked guitar riffs fill out the Southbound sound.

Drums & Vocals

Tony Vivilacqua

At age 11 Tony got his first sticks and Folgers cans. Playing along with classic rock on KZAP, he turned pro at 16. Cutting his teeth on Skynyrd, Allmans, and Molly Hatchet gave him the authentic chops needed to drive Southbound's rhythm.

Keyboards, Guitar & Vocals

Larry Audino

Larry began on piano at age 10 and picked up guitar to gig with friends. By age 19, he brought Hammond organ and keyboard prowess to top regional bands. An integral part of Savannah Blue and now the keyboard backbone of Southbound.

The Live Experience

Southbound doesn't just play covers — they honor the legacy of Southern Rock with the kind of commitment that only comes from musicians who genuinely love this music. Every show features the twin-lead guitar harmonies, the B-3 organ fills, and the three-part vocal blends that made these songs timeless.
