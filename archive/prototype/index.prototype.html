The Southbound Sound

Performing regularly throughout Northern California and the Greater Sacramento/Sierra area since 2018.

Southbound is Northern California's premier Southern Rock Revue. We deliver the authentic grit, twin-lead guitar harmonies, and soulful vocals of Southern Rock classics with an upbeat sound that keeps dance floors packed.

Our music is suitable for all ages and appeals to a wide audience—young and old alike. Accustomed to playing to large crowds, Southbound is a regular headline participant in regional music festivals, concerts in the park, car/motorcycle rallies, corporate affairs, and premier live music venues.

The Lineup

Meet The Musicians

Seasoned veterans with decades of brotherhood and stage chemistry, bringing true Southern Rock authority to every show.

Lead Vocals

Scott Baxter

A multi-instrumentalist and veteran of hundreds of shows along the Texas Gulf coast, including gigs with rock and roll pioneer Bo Diddley and South Texas blues legend Rocky Benton. Scott has been the powerhouse front-man and voice of Southbound since 2023.

Guitars & Vocals

Dean Fisher

The release of Freebird nearly 50 years ago triggered Dean's desire to pick up guitar. Longtime partner with Mike Cochran; they formed Savannah Blue 28 years ago and brought that brotherhood into Southbound. To play music and ride with your brothers is Dean's idea of living the dream!

Bass & Vocals

Mike Cochran

Decided at age 15 he was going to play bass. Ten years later, he and Dean Fisher began jamming at backyard BBQs, forming Savannah Blue. 28 years later, Mike and Dean morphed those Southern Rock influences into Southbound: "I've been very fortunate to play with incredible musicians and learn from every one."

Guitar & Vocals

Rusty Chain

Growing up outside Washington DC in a vibrant Blues/Rock scene (Danny Gatton, Jimmy Thackery & the Nighthawks), Rusty gigged throughout MD, DC, and Virginia. After relocating to Sacramento in the late 90s, he formed the Chain-Lightning Band and Bad Catz. Today his gritty, blues-soaked riffs fill out the Southbound sound.

Drums & Vocals

Tony Vivilacqua

At age 11 Tony received his first wood sticks, Folgers coffee cans, and a radio before buying his first $25 drum set from Auburn Journal classifieds. Playing along with classic rock KZAP, Tony turned pro at 16. Cutting teeth on Skynyrd, Allman Brothers, and Molly Hatchet gave him the chops needed for Southbound!

Keys, Guitar & Vocals

Larry Audino

Began playing piano at 10 and picked up guitar for easy hauling before keyboard technology advanced at age 19. Southern Rock has always been his favorite genre; Larry was an integral founding keyboardist with Savannah Blue and now provides the rich Hammond organ and piano backbone of Southbound.
